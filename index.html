Participant reference guide

AI scribe technology
comparison

Eleven options for ambient clinical documentation, grouped by EHR fit and workflow. This page expands on the presentation slide and links to the product sources.

An AI scribe listens to a clinical encounter and drafts documentation for a clinician to review. Capabilities and the depth of EHR integration differ by product and deployment.

ProductBest fitIntegration and capability
Epic AI Charting
EHR native		Epic organizationsPart of Epic’s Art suite. Drafts visit notes and queues suggested orders within Epic.
Oracle Health Clinical AI Agent
EHR native		Oracle Health organizationsDrafts notes and orders in Oracle Health workflows; note generation also supports US emergency and inpatient settings.
athenaAmbient
EHR native		athenaOne practicesBuilt into athenaOne. Drafts notes, potential diagnoses, and recommended order types. athenahealth says it is available at no additional cost.
Sunoh.aieClinicalWorks practices; other ambulatory EHR usersAmbient notes closely integrated with eClinicalWorks. A standalone mobile app is available to users of other EHRs.
AbridgeHealth systems seeking broad deploymentDocumentation across multiple care settings, with EHR integrations including Epic, Oracle Health, and athenahealth. Won Best in KLAS Ambient Speech in 2025 and 2026.
Microsoft Dragon CopilotOrganizations using dictation and ambient documentationCombines dictation, ambient notes, and AI workflows. Features available inside the EHR depend on that EHR’s integration.
SukiPractices and systems using multiple EHRsAmbient notes and voice workflows. Suki names deep integrations with Epic, Oracle Health, athenahealth, and MEDITECH.
DeepScribeSpecialty practices and health systemsSpecialty-focused documentation and coding support; Epic integration includes Haiku and Canto.
Ambience HealthcareHealth systems seeking documentation and coding supportAmbient notes, coding, and documentation tools; integrations include Epic, Oracle/Cerner, and athenahealth.
NablaPractices and systems seeking flexible deploymentAmbient notes and dictation. Its Epic integration supports notes, schedule synchronization, and mobile workflows.
HeidiIndividual clinicians and teamsAmbient notes with free and paid plans. Eligible paid plans provide EHR integration, including Epic.
Questions to ask in a demo: Which EHR version and clinical settings are supported? Does the product write structured data back to the chart or transfer note text? What review is required before a note or order is signed? Ask each vendor for a written quote covering implementation, integration, usage, and support.
